ADMISSIONS

Become part of a focused learning community

We welcome learners who are ready to learn with purpose, respect the school community and commit to academic and personal growth. Admission is guided by the school’s intake policy, available places and the applicant’s readiness for a structured secondary education.

Who can apply

Prospective students seeking a supportive environment for learning, character development and achievement are encouraged to apply.

Application process

Parents or guardians are advised to contact the school office for guidance, available vacancies and the next steps in the admission process.

School visit

Visiting the campus helps families understand the environment, meet the staff and make an informed decision about enrolment.

For the latest verified intake information, fees and required documents, please contact the school administration directly.
