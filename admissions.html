Admission process

Join a school that values excellence and character.

How to apply

Simple steps to a strong start.

1

Submit an enquiry

Contact the school office or visit us to learn about available places and entry requirements.

2

Complete application

Provide academic details, parent or guardian information and the required documents.

3

Review and placement

Our admissions team reviews the application and confirms the next steps for enrolment.

Entry requirements

We look for readiness, potential and commitment.

📄

Academic records

Previous school reports and achievement records are reviewed as part of the process.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Parent engagement

We value a shared commitment between parents, students and the school community.

🧑‍🏫

School readiness

Students should be ready for a disciplined, enthusiastic and academically focused environment.

Documents needed

Prepare the essentials before applying.

  • Birth certificate or proof of age
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Transfer letter or previous school records where applicable
  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Any supporting academic documents
