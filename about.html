ABOUT THE SCHOOL

Shaping confident, disciplined learners

Central College Kawempe is a vibrant secondary school committed to academic excellence, character formation and responsible citizenship. We provide a structured environment where learners are encouraged to pursue knowledge, grow in confidence and become active contributors to their families, communities and the nation.

Our mission

To provide quality education that develops knowledge, skills, discipline and integrity in a safe, motivating and supportive environment.

Our vision

To be a centre of excellence in secondary education, nurturing learners who are prepared for higher education and impactful leadership.

Our values

Discipline, respect, excellence, teamwork and integrity guide our daily learning, conduct and relationships across the school community.

At Central College Kawempe, we believe every learner deserves a strong academic foundation, a caring community and the confidence to pursue a meaningful future.
