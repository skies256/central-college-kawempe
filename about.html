Who we are

Central College Kawempe is a learning community grounded in excellence.

Since its foundation, Central College Kawempe has remained committed to building a school culture that values academic focus, discipline, honesty and respect. We believe every learner is capable of growth when given structure, inspiration and support.

“We are not only preparing students for examinations, but for leadership, service and citizenship in a changing world.”
